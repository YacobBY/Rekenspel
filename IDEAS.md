# Math Game Ideas — girls 6–9, Dutch curriculum (groep 3–5)

Generated 2026-08-24 from 4 ideation lenses + web-verified Dutch curriculum research. Target: math is a major, wanted part of the game — never a bolt-on quiz. Session shape guideline: ~1/3 play, ~2/3 math, and play is never locked behind a correct answer — math buys *better*, never *permission*.

## Curriculum anchor (rekenen-wiskunde NL)

New kerndoelen 10–18 in force since Aug 2026 (overgangsrecht until 2031); per-groep detail still comes from SLO Tussendoelen 2017. Key ladder:

| | Groep 3 (6–7) | Groep 4 (7–8) | Groep 5 (8–9) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Number domain | tot 20 (orient tot 100) | tot 100 | tot 1000 |
| Memorized (2s recall OK) | splitsingen + optellen/aftrekken t/m 10 | +/− t/m 20; tafels 1,2,3,4,5,10 | ALL tafels t/m 10 |
| Strategies | via de 5 / via de 10 | rijgen (64−27→44−7), splitsen (30+20, 4+7), compenseren | verdeelstrategie (8×36=8×30+8×6), delen met rest, cijferen starts |
| Klok | hele uren (halve = stretch) | hele/halve/kwartieren, analog↔digital | op de minuut + tijdsduur berekenen |
| Geld | €1/€2/€5/€10, bedragen t/m €20 | all coins/notes; cents only t/m €2; whole euros tot €100 | komma-notatie (€4,95), wisselgeld van €50 |
| Meten | natural units, compare | m + cm, omtrek, kg | mm/dm/km, liter/ml, oppervlakte on grid, prijs per kg |
| Meetkunde/data | shapes, staafdiagram read | spiegelbeeld, aanzichten, turven | lijnsymmetrie, bouwplaat, patterns, legenda |

Design traps to avoid:
- `7×8` is groep 5, NOT groep 4 (groep 4 memorizes only tafels 1–5 and 10).
- `€3,45` comma notation is groep 5; groep 4 says "3 euro en 45 cent"; groep-4 cent composition only t/m €2.
- No cijferen (column arithmetic) before groep 5 — conflicts with school.
- 2-second timers only on *memoriseren* items; strategy-year skills get ~3–5s or untimed, and never penalize a slower correct strategy.
- Align with school models: rekenrek (vijfstructuur), lege getallenlijn, splits-schema, verhoudingstabel. Realistic contexts (winkel, recept, kassabon) are what kerndoelen 15/16/18 demand.
- Money realism: no €200/€500 notes.

Full source list in worker report (SLO kerndoelen PDF 2025, Tussendoelen PO 2017, TULE, Noordhoff Reken Zeker, LOWAN). Re-check SLO leerlijnen before locking long-term difficulty tiers — new per-groep leerlijnen still in development.

## Top picks (girls 6–9, curriculum fit)

### 1. Silverhoof — Trail Season ⭐ (your horse idea, evolved)
Autoplay cross-country run (60–90s) with named, custom-colored horse; child taps flourishes (perfect-jump ribbons, cheer uphill). Run ends with a **diagnosis**: "Juniper slipped on the water jump" — and the shop visibly sells the cure (studded shoes). Three math stations (~2/3 of session): **tack shop** (drag real coins, pay + change), **feed bench** (split bale into equal feeds, mix 2:1 oats/bran), **training board** (partition 10 hours over jumping/stamina/speed = stat allocation). Purchases appear on horse immediately, act next run — anticipation engine.
- Difficulty ladder = the curriculum ladder: coins to 10 → make 20 → totals/change tot 100 → budget wishlist → fractions of feed. Carries one child groep 3 → 5.
- Failure: shopkeeper counts along ("that's seven — two more?"), coins stay on counter, no red X, barn never locked.
- Skills: geld, splitsen, +/− tot 100, delen, breuken. **Groep 3–5.**

### 2. Waggletail Lane Rescue
Backyard animal rescue (puppies, kittens, one opinionated goose). Portion food by drag-to-share (12 biscuits ÷ 4 puppies, remainder to treat jar), schedule walks on 5-min clock strip between 2:00–4:00, decide intake: 20 scoops, 4/day used, delivery in 4 days — can you accept the puppy who eats 3? Healthy animals get adopted in a warm ceremony; letters from new families pin to your wall.
- Failure: droopy animal, vet lays out correct split as worked example; nothing dies; replay any day free.
- Skills: eerlijk delen, delen met rest, herhaald optellen, klokkijken + tijdsduur. **Groep 4–5 core, entry at groep 3.**

### 3. Nibblewick — Nest of Small Dragons
Pocket creature you name and tickle; it craves food via picture bubbles. Cook on a **ten-frame plate**: build 5 as 3+2, match plates, double a portion, share 8 fish between two dragons. No currency at all. **Diet determines evolution** — berry diet grows a round magenta glider, fish diet a sleek blue one; collection book with silhouette gaps.
- This is splitsen t/m 10 memoriseren — THE groep 3 core skill — as a kitchen.
- Failure: curious sniff, "that's four — I wanted five", glowing empty slot. Never sad, never a clock.
- Skills: subitizing, splitsen, bonds to 10/20, doubling, fair share. **Groep 3 (ages 5–7).**

### 4. Moth & Lantern — A Garden at Dusk
Design a garden on a grid; at dusk creatures visit what you actually planted and you photograph them for an album. Flower beds are **arrays** (4×3=12 bulbs), fencing costs by **omtrek**, soil by **oppervlakte**, seed packs of 6 for 20 bulbs = delen met rest, symmetry for formal beds. Album gaps publish geometry requirements: "Emerald Hawkmoth needs one connected bed of ≥12 night-bloomers."
- Math is the design tool, never a ticket; infinite undo; creature "hasn't come *yet*".
- Skills: tafels as arrays, omtrek vs oppervlakte, delen met rest, symmetrie, skip counting. **Groep 4–5.**

### 5. Bramblewood Post
Postal fox village round (autoplay, cozy, gossip threads). Sorting room: balance parcels to target weight, pack cart under capacity limit (multiple valid answers), split 24 pastries across 5 houses — *and the game asks who gets the extra* — make change for stamps. Named residents remember what you brought; desire is relational, not acquisitive.
- Failure: overloaded cart wobbles, one parcel bounces into hedge, delivered next round. Nobody is ever angry.
- Skills: wegen/vergelijken, sums to target, delen met rest, geld/wisselgeld. **Groep 3–5 (ages 6–9 exact).**

### 6. Foldlings
Finger-paint half a creature on wet paper, pinch to fold, open — mirrored creature walks into your meadow. Repair mode: repaint a faded half; **asymmetric creatures limp in circles**. Fold line moves to diagonal, double folds → rotational symmetry.
- Zero text, pre-reader safe; cheapest build (2D canvas + mirrored blit).
- Skills: lijnsymmetrie/spiegelen (explicit groep 4 spiegelbeeld + groep 5 lijnsymmetrie goals). **Ages 4–9.**

## Second tier (good, keep on list)

- **Muddy-Boot Market Garden** — vegetable stall: count out orders, compose coins, "enough/not enough" purse screen. Pure groep 3 geld (whole euros tot €20). Named regulars + duckling savings target.
- **Two Buckets** — balance-beam ferry; ducklings only cross a *level* beam. Live equals sign; mystery sacks = missing addend. Groep 3.
- **Snailpost** — lay numbered planks whose length IS the number to bridge exact gaps for a sweet slow snail. Splitsen/missing addend tot 20. Groep 3.
- **Furrow** — plant 24 seeds as 4×6 / 3×8 / 4×4+4×2 around rocks: factor pairs + distributive property by obstacle, discovered while decorating your garden. Groep 4–5.
- **Hay & Miles** — pony cart provisioning: compute days × bales before departure, shop never checks your math, **the road spends what you brought** (run dry → arrive after dark). Second horse game; grader is the world, not a quiz. Groep 4–5.
- **Tallybird** — wildlife photographer; build your own bar charts from photo stickers, re-bin by dragging axis, chart = treasure map to rare birds. Data goals groep 4–5 (turven, staafdiagram, legenda).
- **Tintworks** — dye house: mix 3:1 blue:yellow, scale recipes ×3, name discovered colors ("Frog Pond"). High girl appeal; ratio math sits top of groep 5 and beyond — candidate for the older follow-up.

## Shared engine (feasibility for building multiple)

All top picks run on two reusable modules:
1. **Adaptive difficulty**: rolling accuracy + hesitation over last ~10 items; promote on sustained ease, silently soften on struggle; never show level numbers; separate timer policy for memoriseren (2s) vs inzicht (untimed).
2. **Manipulative input**: drag objects onto a target surface with live running total — serves coin counter, feed bench, ten-frame plate, grid, parcel scale.

Anti-dark-pattern rules across all: no energy meters, no countdowns-as-pressure, no streak guilt, no "your creature is sad because you left", no purchase shortcuts past the math.

Suggested build order if making several: **Silverhoof** first (proves engine + your seed idea), then **Nibblewick** (groep 3 entry, reuses plate/coin manipulative), then **Moth & Lantern** or **Bramblewood Post** (groep 4–5 depth).
